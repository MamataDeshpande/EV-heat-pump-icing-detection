# Icing Detection on Outside Heat Exchanger (OHX) Using Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence

**Master's Thesis | Automotive Software Engineering | Technische Universität Chemnitz**  
**Research at Mercedes-Benz AG**

## Project Overview

The outside heat exchanger (OHX) is an important part of an electric vehicle's heat-pump system. Under cold and humid conditions, ice can accumulate on its surface, restricting airflow and reducing heat-transfer efficiency. This can affect cabin heating performance and overall energy consumption.

My master's research investigated a machine-learning-based approach to identifying icing conditions in automotive heat-pump systems. The work focused on understanding the thermal-management problem and exploring whether patterns in recorded vehicle measurements could support icing detection.

## Research Work

The project involved the analysis of automotive measurement data, development of a proof-of-concept classification approach using Python and machine learning, and evaluation of the approach within the available research conditions.

The study demonstrated the potential of data-driven methods for thermal-system diagnostics. Further validation across different environmental and vehicle operating conditions would be required before any production application.

## Research Outcome

The work contributed to research associated with a **published Mercedes-Benz patent application**. This project is presented here as a high-level overview of my master's research.
